# Battle Royale Decision Atlas

This is the authoritative product-intent record for Battle Royale as the first MMORPG PvP mode. Entries are stable and append-only; a later decision supersedes an earlier one explicitly. Implemented status is tracked by code and issues, not by assuming these decisions have shipped.

- **1** Product intent
  - **1.1** [owner decision; implementation pending] Battle Royale is a **fantasy PvP activity inside the broader MMORPG**, not a separately designed gun shooter. It uses **full-scale MMORPG characters**, their real models, class abilities, weapons, spells, movement and combat. A separate repository may retain Battle Royale-specific rules and server composition, but it is one gameplay mode of the MMORPG. ([mmorpg Decision Atlas 5.5.15](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/main/docs/decisions/atlas.md); owner direction, 2026-10-19.)
  - **1.2** [owner decision; implementation pending] Every participant can immediately try a **fully leveled character at the MMORPG's currently supported maximum level**, with legal level-appropriate gear and currently implemented class abilities; no MMO leveling/grinding prerequisite. Reuse MMORPG class, appearance, abilities and actual combat mechanics instead of inventing Battle Royale equivalents. Match-local testing must not implicitly change persistent character records. ([mmorpg #340](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/340); owner direction, 2026-10-19.)
  - **1.3** [owner decision; implementation pending] Character and equipment choices can change **between lives**, never during an active life. Under the existing single-elimination Battle Royale lifecycle, that means after elimination and before another match, not an in-round respawn mechanic. ([mmorpg #341](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/341); owner direction, 2026-10-19.)
  - **1.4** [owner decision; implementation pending] Deliver **one mode first: Battle Royale**, playable with friends and eventually more participants. Additional PvP modes and their exact rules, and unsettled Battle Royale details such as loot, teams, rewards, respawn variants, drop mechanics, balancing and match timing, remain open for later design; do not pre-build them now. The current deterministic lobby/drop/combat/finished and storm behavior are an implementation foundation, not a new product commitment to every placeholder value. ([battle-royale #13](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/battle-royale/issues/13); owner direction, 2026-10-19.)
- **2** Implementation ownership
  - **2.1** [owner decision; implementation pending] `battle-royale-core` may import a pinned `mmorpg-core` and compose **one `mmorpg_core::ZoneSimulation`** per authoritative match for movement, combat, class abilities, equipment, health and physics. Do not keep the old duplicate player simulator alongside it. PvP eligibility and hits belong to the shared MMORPG combat implementation. ([mmorpg #339](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/339); [battle-royale #13](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/battle-royale/issues/13).)
  - **2.2** [owner decision; implementation pending] Battle Royale retains authority only over mode-specific admission, lifecycle, safe zone, elimination, victory and any future mode-only rules. `game-server` continues to own sessions, command sequences, tick/reconnect and recovery; canonical match recovery includes the entire composited MMORPG zone state, not a mirrored second source of truth. Existing single-writer, replay and visibility invariants remain in force. ([battle-royale #13](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/battle-royale/issues/13).)
  - **2.3** [owner decision; implementation pending] Prefer the existing MMORPG clients and their proven character rendering, animation, input and asset foundations for the user-facing PvP entry and play path. The first usable slice must prove real two-client MMORPG PvP rather than only a server-only or mocked Pages demonstration. Do not add a separate graphics/asset stack for this mode. ([mmorpg #341](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/341).)

  - **2.4** [decision; server composition implemented in #13] Implementing 2.1–2.3 at the authoritative mode boundary: one ephemeral free-for-all MMORPG zone owns every character; the mode retains its existing phase/storm schedule and records terminal eliminations and winner. Hosts may admit a preselected build; transport guests may configure one during Waiting, with setup locked at Drop. Protocol/schema/ruleset v2 embed existing MMORPG codecs and complete zone recovery. A headless recovery/reconnect scenario and real two-client hosted fight prove this server/client contract; rendered MMORPG entry remains MMORPG #341, and no 100-player network/rendering readiness is claimed.
  - **2.5** [owner decision; graybox implemented in #31] The first mode's single arena is a **symmetric multilevel fantasy fortress**: central courtyard, interior rooms/corridors, stairs, towers, rooftops, elevated routes and several entrances, with **traversal-only** moving platforms (no traps or damaging hazards). `battle-royale-core::arena` defines its collision once as shared `mmorpg-core` content (boxes, ramps, platforms) owned by the zone's Rapier physics; MMORPG presentation consumes that content and its revision rather than a copy ([ARENA.md](../ARENA.md)). Graybox first; authored art later through the existing Blender/asset-tooling path. Loot, teams, storm tuning, drop mechanics and spawn distribution stay unchanged and open.
